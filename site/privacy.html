Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ByteFlow is built so that your files, keys and credentials stay with you. We don't run a server that sees your data, and there is no account to create.

The ByteFlow app

  • Your files go only to your own bucket. The app talks directly to the Cloudflare R2 bucket you connect. Uploads, downloads, listings and share links travel between your computer and your bucket, and never through us.
  • Credentials and keys stay on your computer. Your R2 access keys, encryption keys and passwords are stored locally, sealed under your login password and Windows data protection. They are never sent to us.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports. The app doesn't track usage, doesn't send diagnostics and doesn't write logs to disk.
  • Update check. When it starts, the app asks GitHub for the latest release (latest.json on the ByteFlow releases page). That request contains no information about you or your vault. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request. If you choose to download an update, the installer is also fetched from GitHub.
  • Local data. A listing cache, thumbnails and settings are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ByteFlow on your computer. Settings → Danger zone → "Wipe everything" erases them. Uninstalling the app keeps them, so a reinstall picks up where you left off.

Cloudflare and GitHub

Your bucket is hosted by Cloudflare under your own Cloudflare account, so Cloudflare's privacy policy and your agreement with Cloudflare apply to what's stored there. Encrypted files are encrypted on your computer with AES-256-GCM before upload, so Cloudflare only holds ciphertext for them. Downloads and the update check are served by GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard technical request logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

Children

ByteFlow isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.
